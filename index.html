Crush Room

The walls on both sides are about to slide together. Pick the one spot where you won't get flattened.

Bump-out: sticks out into the room
Recess: set back into the wall
Pillar: a wall that touches it stops
  • Tap the row where you want to stand. In a row with a pillar, tap the side of the pillar you want.
  • Both walls slide in at the same speed. A wall that hits a pillar stops right there. The other wall keeps coming until it hits a pillar too, or the stopped wall.
  • You fill one square. You survive if at least one square of your row (or your side of the pillar) is still uncovered at the end.
  • Take your time, but faster picks score more. One wrong pick ends the run.
